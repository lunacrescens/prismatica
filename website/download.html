Seeing a security warning?

Prismatica isn't signed with a paid developer certificate yet. On Windows, choose More info › Run anyway. On a Mac, open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. On Linux, if it won't start, the README.txt inside the download has a one-line fix.
